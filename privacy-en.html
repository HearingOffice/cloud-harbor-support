CLOUD HARBOR

Privacy policy

Overview

This policy describes Cloud Harbor, including optional advertising where enabled. Contact the developer at support@elevife.com. Updated 4 October 2026.

Your game stays on your device

Game progress, settings, age category, and local reward receipts are stored on your device. The game does not require an account and does not upload saves, contacts, photos, or precise location. Exported backups are shared only with the destination you choose.

Network services

The app may retrieve operational configuration from a Neon-hosted API. It does not send player analytics in the current version. Hosting and distribution providers may process ordinary connection information such as IP addresses and technical logs to deliver and protect their services.

Optional advertising

Eligible players aged 16 or older can choose rewarded ads. Before requesting an ad, Google User Messaging Platform updates applicable consent choices. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Publisher-provided personalization is disabled. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Declining a choice never blocks basic play. Google may offer non-personalized or limited ads depending on consent and region.

Choices and retention

Ad privacy choices can be reopened in Settings when Google requires that option. Selecting under 16 or leaving age undeclared disables new ad requests. Local game data remains until you erase it or remove the app; device backups you create may remain elsewhere. Provider logs and advertising data follow the applicable provider policies and account settings. Support correspondence is used to answer your request; you may ask us to delete it.

Children and regional rights

No ad request is made for players who select under 16. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through player accounts because no account system is offered. Contact support@elevife.com to request access, correction, deletion, or other rights applicable in your region, or to report a child privacy concern. Do not send unnecessary personal information.

Providers and updates

Apple distributes the app, Neon hosts configuration services, and Google provides optional ads and consent forms. These providers may process data internationally. Their policies are linked below. We will update this page when practices change.
